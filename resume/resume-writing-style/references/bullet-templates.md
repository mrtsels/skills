# Resume Bullet Templates

10 template formulas for equity research / asset management / PE / VC resume bullets. Each includes the formula and a concrete example.

## 1. Industry Research (most通用)

**Formula:** Conducted in-depth research on [industry], analyzing [market size / competitive landscape / regulations / trends] through [data sources], and identified [key findings].

**Example:** Conducted in-depth research on China's EV industry, analyzing market size, competitive dynamics, and policy developments through company filings and industry reports, identifying key growth drivers and investment risks.

## 2. Company / Equity Research

**Formula:** Evaluated [Company] by analyzing financial statements, operating metrics, valuation multiples, and competitive positioning to assess investment attractiveness.

**Example:** Evaluated BYD and CATL through financial statement analysis, operating metrics, and peer valuation comparisons to assess long-term earnings potential.

## 3. Competitive Benchmarking

**Formula:** Compared [number] leading companies across [metrics], benchmarking business models, profitability, market share, and valuation.

**Example:** Compared eight semiconductor companies across revenue growth, gross margin, market share, and valuation multiples to identify relative investment opportunities.

## 4. Financial Modeling

**Formula:** Built a [3-statement / DCF / comparable] valuation model using financial projections and sensitivity analysis to estimate intrinsic value.

**Example:** Built a discounted cash flow model with sensitivity analysis under multiple growth scenarios to estimate intrinsic equity value.

## 5. Data Analysis

**Formula:** Collected and analyzed [data] using [Excel / Python / SQL / Bloomberg / Wind], generating actionable insights on [topic].

**Example:** Collected and analyzed industry shipment data using Python and Excel to evaluate demand trends and forecast market growth.

## 6. Policy Research

**Formula:** Monitored policy and regulatory developments affecting [industry], assessing their implications for industry growth and company performance.

## 7. Competitive Landscape / Positioning

**Formula:** Assessed competitive positioning by examining product portfolios, pricing strategies, channel expansion, and market share evolution.

## 8. Expert Interviews / Earnings Calls

**Formula:** Synthesized insights from [expert interviews / earnings calls / conference presentations] to validate investment assumptions and industry outlook.

## 9. Report Output

**Formula:** Produced comprehensive research reports covering industry outlook, company fundamentals, valuation, catalysts, and investment risks.

## 10. Investment Recommendation

**Formula:** Developed investment recommendations by integrating macroeconomic trends, industry analysis, financial modeling, and valuation.

## 11. Daily Market Briefing (AM/Trust)

**Formula:** Delivered daily market briefings using [specific indicators: index breadth ratios, sector capital flow rankings, technical indicators with names, factor attribution method] for [purpose]; executed [trading volume/frequency] on [platform] with [total volume] across [markets].

**Key rules:**
- Lead with analysis, follow with execution.
- Name specific indicators (moving averages, RSI, VaR, factor attribution).
- For intern roles: use "Completed"/"Delivered"/"Executed" — avoid "Orchestrated"/"Managed".
- Include trade volume and market scope.

**Example:** Delivered daily market briefings using index breadth ratios, sector capital flow rankings, and technical indicators (moving averages, RSI, VaR) with factor model attribution for style rotation signals; executed 30+ daily bond repo and reverse repo orders (GC001-GC014) on xQuant OMS with ¥800M+ daily turnover across exchange and interbank markets

## 12. Post-Investment / Fund Monitoring (FoF)

**Formula:** Completed [period] portfolio holdings analysis for [N] products using [specific methods: concentration, sector exposure, NAV trends] and monitored [topic] via [sources]; authored [report type] covering [specific report contents] across [N] fund managers (¥[AUM] total).

**Key rules:**
- Sequence: holdings analysis → negative news screening → report writing.
- Name analysis methods (concentration ratios, sector exposure, NAV trends).
- Specify monitoring method ("via financial news platforms and web searches").
- Report contents must be explicit: "covering market review, holdings analysis, and sub-advisor assessments".
- Include AUM/scale.

**Example:** Completed Q2 portfolio holdings analysis (concentration, sector exposure, NAV trends) for 10+ FoF products and monitored sub-advisor negative news via financial news platforms and web searches; authored post-investment reports covering market review, holdings analysis, and sub-advisor assessments across dozens of partner fund managers (¥10B+ total AUM)
