# Interview Prep Generator

Use when the user has an interview coming up and needs preparation materials.

## Deliverables

1. **STAR Stories**: map resume bullets to STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
2. **Likely Questions**: based on role type, seniority, and company
3. **Talking Points**: key messages to convey for each resume section
4. **Company Research**: what to know about the company before the interview
5. **Questions to Ask**: intelligent questions for the interviewer

## STAR Story Format

- Situation: context (1-2 sentences)
- Task: what needed to be done (1 sentence)
- Action: what you specifically did (2-3 sentences, use active verbs)
- Result: measurable outcome (1-2 sentences, use numbers)

## Question Categories by Type

- Behavioral: Tell me about a time when...
- Technical: role-specific skills assessment
- Situational: What would you do if...
- Resume deep-dive: walk me through your experience with X
