# Cover Letter Generator

Use when the user needs a personalized cover letter for a job application.

## Structure

1. Header: contact info, date, company info
2. Opening: specific role, how you found it, hook (1-2 sentences)
3. Body paragraph 1: relevant achievement that matches top requirement
4. Body paragraph 2: second key achievement with different strength
5. Body paragraph 3: cultural fit and motivation for this company
6. Closing: call to action, confidence statement

## Rules
- Address to specific person if known, otherwise use hiring team
- Reference company-specific details (product, mission, news)
- One page max
- No generic phrases
- Each claim backed by resume evidence
- Match tone to company culture

## Cold Outreach Emails

Use when the user wants to reach out to companies or hiring managers directly.

## Cold Email Structure

1. **Subject Line**: specific, personalized, non-spammy
2. **Opening**: common ground or genuine compliment (1 sentence)
3. **Value Proposition**: what you bring (1-2 sentences)
4. **Specific Ask**: meeting, referral, advice (1 sentence)
5. **Close**: gratitude, easy opt-out

## Rules

- Research the recipient before writing
- Reference something specific (their product, post, talk)
- Keep to 5-7 sentences
- One clear call to action
- Make it easy to say yes
- Follow up after 5-7 days
- Track response rates

## Application Form Responses

Use when the user needs help filling out online job application forms.

## Capabilities

- Parse application form fields
- Extract matching information from resume
- Tailor answers to the specific job
- Handle common field types (text, dropdown, yes/no)
- Track what was submitted

## Common Form Fields

- Contact info: straight from resume
- Work history: formatted for text fields (company, title, dates, bullets)
- Education: degree, institution, dates
- Skills: comma-separated or select from list
- Cover letter / additional info: shorter version of cover letter
- Demographics: optional, answer or skip
- Equal opportunity: standard answers

## Rules

- Never misrepresent information
- Use exact dates from resume
- Match job title to what the form expects
- Keep answers concise (forms have character limits)
- Save a copy of what was submitted
