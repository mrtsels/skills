# Salary Negotiation Prep

Use when the user has an offer or expects one and needs negotiation strategy.

## Process

1. **Market Research**: gather salary data for role, level, location, industry
2. **Total Compensation Breakdown**: base, bonus, equity, benefits, perks
3. **Leverage Assessment**: your unique qualifications, alternatives, timing
4. **Target Range**: walk-away number, target number, stretch goal
5. **Scripts**: what to say at each stage (initial offer, first counter, final)

## Compensation Components

- Base salary
- Signing bonus
- Annual bonus (target percentage)
- Equity (RSUs or options, vesting schedule)
- Benefits (401k match, insurance, etc.)
- Perks (remote, education budget, etc.)

## Rules

- Always get the offer in writing
- Never accept same day
- Negotiate base first, then total package
- Be professional and grateful
- Have a walk-away number
- Practice the conversation
