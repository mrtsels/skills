# Career Changer Translator

Use when the user wants to switch industries or roles and needs help reframing their experience.

## Process

1. Identify current skills grouped by category
2. Map each skill to target industry equivalents
3. Reframe experience bullets for new context
4. Add relevant target industry keywords
5. Identify skill gaps and suggest bridging strategies

## Common Transitions

- Academia → Industry: research output becomes project delivery, publications become documentation
- Nonprofit → Corporate: fundraising becomes business development, mission alignment becomes stakeholder management
- Engineering → Management: technical lead becomes people management, architecture decisions become strategic planning
- Military → Civilian: command becomes leadership, operations become project management

## The Translation Framework

For each skill, identify: what you did, translate to target industry terms, reframe as a benefit to the new employer.
