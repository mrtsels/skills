# Cover Letter Generator

Use when the user needs a personalized cover letter for a job application.

## Structure

1. Header: contact info, date, company info
2. Opening: specific role, how you found it, hook (1-2 sentences)
3. Body paragraph 1: relevant achievement that matches top requirement
4. Body paragraph 2: second key achievement with different strength
5. Body paragraph 3: cultural fit and motivation for this company
6. Closing: call to action, confidence statement

## Rules
- Address to specific person if known, otherwise use hiring team
- Reference company-specific details (product, mission, news)
- One page max
- No generic phrases
- Each claim backed by resume evidence
- Match tone to company culture
