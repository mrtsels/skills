# Executive Resume Writer

Use for C-suite, VP, and senior leadership roles.

## Key Differences from Standard Resumes

- Focus on strategic impact, not tactical tasks
- Emphasize P&L responsibility and business outcomes
- Board-level communication and stakeholder management
- Organizational transformation and culture change
- Revenue, profitability, and growth metrics

## Executive Resume Structure

1. **Executive Summary**: brand statement, key differentiators (3-5 lines)
2. **Core Competencies**: 8-12 strategic skills (2-column list)
3. **Professional Experience**: reverse chronological, 5-7 bullets per role
4. **Board Positions**: if applicable
5. **Education + Executive Education**: include leadership programs
6. **Professional Affiliations**: board memberships, industry associations

## Executive-Level Metrics

- P&L responsibility size ($X revenue, $Y budget)
- Team size and org structure (direct reports, total org)
- Revenue growth or turnaround percentages
- M&A experience (deal size, integration)
- Board presentations and investor relations
- Industry recognition and awards
