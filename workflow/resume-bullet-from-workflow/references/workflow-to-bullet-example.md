# Session Example: Daily Market Analysis → Resume Bullet

Source: 2026-07-28 resumes session. User described a daily 8:30 AM market briefing workflow for Yuecai Trust FoF post-investment role.

## Raw Workflow Narrative

"每天8:00-8:20采集行情数据（指数涨跌、板块资金流向、事件催化），整理成复盘简报，8:30晨会汇报，结合估值表映射FoF持仓影响，同步做投顾负面舆情筛查"

## Distillation Process

1. Core deliverable: 晨会市场复盘简报 (morning meeting market briefing)
2. Cadence: Daily
3. Inputs: 指数行情、板块资金、新闻催化事件、估值表
4. Audience: Investment team morning meeting

## Result

**ZH:** `每日复盘市场行情，覆盖指数涨跌、板块资金流向及事件催化，为8:30晨会投后持仓分析提供依据` (48 chars)

**EN:** `Delivered daily market intelligence briefings on index movements, sector capital flows, event catalysts, and FoF portfolio mapping for 8:30 AM investment meetings` (21 words)

## Style Transformations (EN)

| Before | After | Reason |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Produced | Delivered | More active, implies presentation to audience |
| covering X | on X | Gerund → noun/preposition |
| (no filler) | — | Clean |

| Before | After | Reason |
|--------|-------|--------|
| 采集行情数据开展市场复盘 | 每日复盘市场行情 | 采集+开展 redundant, 复盘 implies both |
